## 1.1.8 (2026-11-03)
Market-data pack hardening (Oodle) and a catalyst log. Additive release: session `schema_version` accepts `1.0.0 | ... | 1.1.7 | 1.1.8`, and a 1.1.8 session record gets exactly the 1.1.7 checks (**no new session invariants**). `market_snapshot` packs accept `schema_versions: [1.1.7, 1.1.8]`, one version per pack (mixed packs fail MD-11-PACK). **1.1.7 packs validate exactly as before, with the same error strings and exit codes.** This is asserted against the frozen 1.1.7 loader/validator in `_v1_1_7_backup/` on every 1.1.7 fixture, on hundreds of mutated 1.1.7 documents and on mutated 1.1.7 packs (including a 1.1.7 pack carrying `catalysts.json`, still MD-01-NAME + MD-02-EXTRA). Session records of every old version are likewise identical to the frozen 1.1.7 validator. The new fields below are absent/forbidden in 1.1.7 documents (a 1.1.7 document carrying them fails MD-03-SCHEMA, as before) and required or validated in 1.1.8 documents.
- **`robots_only_no_terms` basis (1.1.8).** A `sources_terms` entry may say the site publishes no terms and only robots.txt was honoured; `terms_url` is then forbidden (schema).
- **`sources_not_used` (1.1.8, index.json, optional).** Sources considered and deliberately not used, with a reason (`terms_forbid`, `login_required`, `paid`, `contact_email_required`, `other`; `other` needs a note) and a note. Never an e-mail address (MD-09). A name in both `sources_terms` and `sources_not_used` is **MD-12-CONFLICT** (new).
- **Per-field `as_of_basis` (1.1.8).** `quote_time | last_trade | fetch_minus_delay | published`. Required on every price and option-chain field that carries a value (`as_of_basis.required_for`: last_price, iv, implied_move, open_interest, volume, bid, ask, spread, spread_pct_of_mid, prior_close): missing is **MD-13-MISSING**; a `fetch_minus_delay` basis on a non-delayed field is **MD-13-DELAY**. `last_trade` is documented as the conservative fallback when a page shows no quote time. iv_rank and earnings fields do not need it.
- **Delayed chain fields fixed in the EXAMPLE pack + MD-14.** Every field sourced from a `sources_terms` entry declared `delayed: true, delay_minutes: N` must itself say `delayed: true` with `delay_minutes` ≥ N: otherwise **MD-14-DELAY** (new, cross-file rule; the schema carries the declaration).
- **Earnings independence by origin (1.1.8, MD-07).** Each earnings source carries `origin` (`issuer | exchange | broker | data_vendor | news | other`) and `origin_entity`. Two sources count as independent only if they differ in origin+origin_entity (not merely domain). Issuer channels (the issuer's IR site, GlobeNewswire, PR Newswire, Business Wire, gcs-web) are one source: an issuer-channel URL with a non-issuer origin is **MD-07-ORIGIN** (new); an issuer's `origin_entity` must be the ticker. 1.1.7 packs keep the 1.1.7 domain rule.
- **`earnings_time` stays UNCONFIRMED unless two independent sources give it.** A session inferred from a conference-call time goes in `summary.md` only, never in a field.
- **`catalysts.json` (1.1.8 only, optional, `kind: catalyst_log`).** Catalysts (`catalyst_type` earnings, fomc, macro_release, investor_day, conference, fda, analyst_action, other; ticker or null for macro) with `event_date`, an optional offset `event_time`, status CONFIRMED / UNCONFIRMED / UNKNOWN (CONFIRMED needs two sources with distinct origin+origin_entity) and sources, plus `ticker_context` (sector, prior_close, analyst actions). Whitelisted in the pack layout for 1.1.8 only, listed in `pack.sha256`, loaded by `load_pack` as `PackResult.catalysts`. New **MD-15-UNKNOWN / -VALUE / -UNSOURCED / -UNCONFIRMED / -TIME-NAIVE / -TIME-INVALID / -DATE / -DUPLICATE**.
- **Doc note: MD-05 is not R01.** The pack's 20-minute STALE limit is measured at `pack_built_at`; the risk rule R01's 15-minute limit is measured at decision time. A pack field that is OK in the pack can still fail R01 later.
- **Validator CLI:** the OK line names the rules version and the pack's (or document's) own schema_version, e.g. `valid (market_snapshot 1.1.8 rules, schema_version 1.1.7, pack rules MD-00..MD-15)`; exit codes are unchanged, and error output on 1.1.7 packs is identical to 1.1.7.
- **Loader:** the index is peeked to choose the version and then re-checked against the verified bytes (TOCTOU); `sources[].as_of` is MD-04-checked in 1.1.8 packs only.
- **Fixtures:** the 1.1.7 packs moved to `fixtures/market_data_v1_1_7/` (built by `build_market_data_fixtures_v1_1_7.py`, byte-identical to the 1.1.7 release); `fixtures/market_data/` now holds 1.1.8 packs with the new cases missing_basis, undeclared_delay, earnings_same_origin, catalysts_bad and catalysts_in_1_1_7.
- New tests `test_market_snapshot_v1_1_8.py` and `test_release_v1_1_8.py`. Frozen 1.1.7 release: `_v1_1_7_backup/` (excluded from collection and from the manifest).

- **New `market_snapshot` schema and rules MD-00..MD-12.** `market_snapshot.schema.json` covers a `ticker_snapshot` (one file per ticker per day) and a `pack_index`. `market_data_rules.yaml` is machine-readable and wins over its md companion `market_data_rules.md`; `staleness_limit_minutes: 20` is defined only there. Every data field is `{value, source, as_of, delayed, delay_minutes, status}` with status OK / STALE / UNKNOWN / UNCONFIRMED. `additionalProperties` is false everywhere. Rules: layout and file safety (MD-01); per-date manifest (MD-02); schema (MD-03); times with an offset, naive is an error (MD-04); OK quotes at most 20 min old, with exactly 20:00 OK and 20:00.001 STALE (MD-05); null means UNKNOWN (MD-06); earnings OK needs ≥ 2 distinct registrable domains (MD-07); delay (MD-08); no e-mail addresses (MD-09); street tickers are ideas only (MD-10); pack consistency (MD-11); optional sources-and-terms note (MD-12); fail closed on a crash (MD-00). iv_rank UNKNOWN is normal and is not an error.

The 1.1.6 precedence test now reads the frozen 1.1.6 list from `_v1_1_6_backup/`.

- **INV-05 (1.1.3) flag acknowledgement:** an approval's `acknowledged_flags` must equal the review's flagged rule_ids (FLAG and soft-UNKNOWN results ∪ recorded `flag_rule_ids`): `INV-05-ACK-MISSING` (an absent field acknowledges nothing), `INV-05-ACK-EXTRA` (a PASS review needs `[]`), `INV-05-ACK-DUPLICATE`. Also `INV-05-VETO` (recorded VETO, a VETO that the results give, or no results), `INV-05-FOREIGN` (review of another ticket), `INV-05-TERMS` (legs/qty/limit), `INV-05-EXPIRY` (`expires_at` must be after `approved_at`).
- **INV-06 (1.1.3) approval status at fills:** `approvals[].status` is canonical. A `revoked` / `expired` / `consumed` approval whose `status_changed_at` is at or before an opening fill invalidates that fill (`INV-06-STATUS`); a non-active approval without `status_changed_at` is `INV-06-STATUS-UNDATED`. Closing fills are not limited by status (a `consumed` stamp after the last opening fill is fine). The approval must be for the position's ticket (`INV-06-TICKET`), match the ticket's terms (`INV-06-TERMS`) and not be on a VETO review (`INV-06-VETO`). Fills before `approved_at` (`INV-06-BEFORE`) or after `expires_at` (`INV-06-EXPIRED`; a fill exactly at `expires_at` is allowed, as before) fail; per leg, side must match and cumulative opening qty ≤ approved quantity × ratio (`INV-06-QTY`); prices respect the approved limit in exact Decimal (`INV-06-LIMIT`).
- **INV-16 (new, 1.1.3) stored verdict = computed verdict:** `overall.verdict`, `blocking_rule_ids` and `flag_rule_ids` must equal `risk_reference.overall(results)` (ids compared as sets, no duplicates): `INV-16-VERDICT` (e.g. a stored PASS over a VETO or hard UNKNOWN), `INV-16-BLOCKING`, `INV-16-FLAGS`, and `INV-16-EMPTY` for a review with no results (also blocked by the schema's `minItems: 1`; defence in depth).
- **`risk_reference.combine([])` is now `UNKNOWN` (fail closed; was `PASS`).** Precedence otherwise unchanged (VETO > UNKNOWN > FLAG > PASS). No YAML case outcome changed (78 risk cases, 45 watcher cases); R09's explicit "no short legs → PASS" is kept. Risk ruleset is now **1.1.3**.

Consumers must treat any non-zero exit, or a missing/unimportable validator, as FAIL.
